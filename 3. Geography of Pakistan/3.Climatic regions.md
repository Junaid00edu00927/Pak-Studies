## 3. Climatic Regions of Pakistan

Pakistan is divided into four primary climatic zones based on altitude, rainfall, and temperature variations:

| Climatic Region | Geographical Areas Included | Climate Characteristics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Highland Climate (Alpine)** | Northern & Northwestern mountains (Gilgit-Baltistan, Chitral, Swat, Murree, Quetta). | Very cold winters with heavy snowfall; mild, short summers; rainfall throughout the year. |
| **2. Lowland Climate (Continental)** | Punjab and Sindh plains (excluding coastal strip). | Extreme continental climate: very hot summers ($45^\circ\text{C}+$), cool winters, monsoon rains during July–August. |
| **3. Arid / Semi-Arid Climate** | Southwestern Balochistan and desert zones (Thar, Cholistan, Kharan). | Hot and dry throughout the year; extreme dust storms; negligible rainfall. |
| **4. Coastal / Maritime Climate** | Coastal strip of Sindh and Balochistan (Karachi, Gwadar, Pasni). | Moderate temperatures year-round due to sea breezes; high humidity; mild winters. |
