## 1. Physiography (Physical Features)

Pakistan's terrain is divided into five main physical regions:

### A. Mountains

* **Northern & Western Mountain Ranges:**
* **Himalayas:** Located in the northeast (includes Nanga Parbat, $8,126\text{ m}$).
* **Karakoram Range:** Located in the north along the Chinese border; home to **K2** ($8,611\text{ m}$, 2nd highest peak in the world) and major glaciers like Siachen and Baltoro.
* **Hindu Kush:** Located in the northwest along the Afghan border (highest peak: Tirich Mir, $7,708\text{ m}$).


* **Western Offshoots:** Safed Koh, Sulaiman Range, and Kirthar Range, forming a natural boundary between the Indus Plain and the Balochistan Plateau.

### B. Plains

* **Upper Indus Plain (Punjab):** Formed by the Indus River and its five eastern tributaries. Features rich alluvial soil and an extensive canal irrigation network.
* **Lower Indus Plain (Sindh):** Extends from Mithankot down to the Indus Delta; features flat land, oxbow lakes, and deltaic swamps.

### C. Deserts

* **Thar Desert:** Located in southeastern Sindh (extension of the Great Indian Desert).
* **Cholistan Desert (Rohi):** Located in southern Punjab.
* **Thal Desert:** Located between the Indus and Jhelum rivers in Punjab.
* **Kharan Desert:** A cold, high-altitude desert located in Balochistan.

### D. Valleys

* **Northern Valleys:** Swat, Hunza, Gilgit, Kaghan, and Skardu—famous for high tourism, glaciers, and mountain agriculture (terraced farming).
* **Peshawar & Kohat Valleys:** Fertile agricultural basins in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

### E. Coastal Areas

* **Sindh Coast:** Features the Indus River Delta, creeks, and mangrove forests. Home to Karachi Port and Port Qasim.
* **Makran Coast (Balochistan):** Rocky and steep coastline extending to the Iranian border; home to Gwadar Deep-Sea Port and Ormara.
