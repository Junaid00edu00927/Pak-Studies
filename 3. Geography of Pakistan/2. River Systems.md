
## 2. River Systems

Pakistan’s river network revolves around the **Indus River System**, which originates near Lake Manasarovar in Tibet, flows through Gilgit-Baltistan, and drains into the Arabian Sea near Karachi.

```
                  [ Indus River (Main Stem) ]
                               |
       +-----------------------+-----------------------+
       |                                               |
[ Western Tributaries ]                         [ Eastern Tributaries ]
  • Kabal River                                   • Jhelum River
  • Kurram River                                  • Chenab River
  • Gomal River                                   • Ravi River
  • Swat River                                    • Sutlej River

```

* **Eastern Tributaries (Join the Indus at Panjnad/Mithankot):**
1. **Jhelum**
2. **Chenab**
3. **Ravi**
4. **Sutlej**


* **Western Tributaries:** Kabul, Swat, Kurram, and Gomal rivers.
* **Indus Waters Treaty (1960):** Mediated by the World Bank between Pakistan and India.
* **Pakistan:** Control over the three western rivers (**Indus, Jhelum, Chenab**).
* **India:** Control over the three eastern rivers (**Ravi, Beas, Sutlej**).
