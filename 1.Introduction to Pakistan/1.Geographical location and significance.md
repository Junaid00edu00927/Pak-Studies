
## 1. Absolute Geographical Location (Coordinates & Area)

* **Latitudinal Location:** $24^\circ 35'$ N to $37^\circ 05'$ N
* **Longitudinal Location:** $61^\circ$ E to $78^\circ$ E
* **Total Area:** $881,913\text{ km}^2$ (including Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir; traditionally cited as $796,095\text{ km}^2$ for the four main provinces).
* **Coastline:** Approximately $1,046\text{ km}$ along the Arabian Sea (South).

---

## 2. Neighboring Countries and Borders

Pakistan is located in **South Asia** at the crossroads of Central Asia, West Asia (Middle East), and East Asia.

| Direction | Neighboring Country / Body | Border Name / Feature | Key Significance |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **East** | India | **Radcliffe Line** (~$2,912\text{ km}$) | Historic link; crucial for regional balance of power in South Asia. |
| **North-East** | China | **Sino-Pak Border** (~$592\text{ km}$) | Karakoram Highway connects the two nations; anchor of CPEC. |
| **North-West** | Afghanistan | **Durand Line** (~$2,640\text{ km}$) | Gateway to Central Asia; Wakhan Corridor separates Pakistan from Tajikistan by a short distance. |
| **West** | Iran | **Pak-Iran Border** (~$909\text{ km}$) | Bridge to the oil-rich Middle East and energy projects (Pak-Iran Gas Pipeline). |
| **South** | Arabian Sea / Indian Ocean | Coastal Belt (Karachi, Gwadar, Ormara) | Strategic sea trade lanes leading toward the Gulf of Oman and Strait of Hormuz. |

## 3. Geo-Strategic & Geopolitical Significance

### A. Pivot of Three Vital Regions

Pakistan connects three major geopolitical regions:

1. **South Asia** (India, Bangladesh, etc.)
2. **Central Asian Republics (CARs)** (Uzbekistan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, etc.)
3. **West Asia / Middle East** (Iran, Gulf countries)

### B. Gateway to Central Asian Republics (CARs)

* Landlocked Central Asian states (rich in natural gas, oil, and minerals) require access to warm waters for global trade.
* Pakistan offers the **shortest sea trade route** to these countries compared to routes via Europe, Russia, or Turkey.

### C. Proximity to Great Powers

Pakistan is situated near major global powers:

* **China** directly to the northeast.
* **Russia** (separated by narrow regional strips through Central Asia).
* Proximity to major global maritime routes, making Pakistan a central player in regional foreign policy and security dynamics.
